Privacy Policy

Best3man Publisher · Operated by 열정의시간 (Time of Passion) · Effective: October 3, 2026

1. What this tool is

Best3man Publisher is a private, internal tool used only by 열정의시간 (Time of Passion) to publish videos to our own YouTube channel @best3___man. It is not offered to the public and has no end users other than the channel owner.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services to upload our own videos, set thumbnails, upload subtitle tracks, update localized titles and descriptions, and post comments on our own channel. By using YouTube content you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information we access, use and store

  • We access only our own channel through OAuth authorized by the channel owner.
  • We store only the video IDs of our own uploads and an OAuth refresh token, kept in a local file on the owner's computer.
  • We do not collect, store, or process personal information of YouTube viewers or any other users.

4. Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose any YouTube API data to third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

Stored data (video IDs and the OAuth token) is kept only while the tool is in use. The channel owner can revoke access at any time via the Google security settings page, after which the stored token is deleted within 30 days.
